- **PolyForm Internal Use** — use for the **internal business operations of you and your company**, at any size. "Your company" includes entities under common control (parents, subsidiaries, affiliates), so a corporate group's own policy management is covered. Note two limits from the license text: you **may not distribute** the software, and it is provided **as is** with no warranty — which is exactly what the two paid options below address.
