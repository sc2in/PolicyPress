PolicyPress 1.5 is out. This release is about making the documents and the site
themselves defensible — accessible to everyone and honest about their own review state.

## What's New

### Opt-in tagged, accessible PDFs (PDF/UA-1)

Set `pdf_standard = "ua-1"` under `[extra.policypress]` and PolicyPress emits PDFs with a
real structure tree, a document title, and image alt text — the accessibility standard many
procurement and government reviews now ask for. It is opt-in because PDF/UA-1 is a hard-fail
standard: to make policies conform, the title page is emitted as a proper level-1 heading so
body headings stay consecutive, and every image carries alt text. A CI gate validates the
demo PDFs against PDF/UA-1 with veraPDF, so a rendering change can't silently break
conformance.

### Overdue reviews are caught at build time

A policy whose `last_reviewed` date is older than `review_overdue_days` (default 365) is now
reported by the build pre-flight — a warning by default, fatal with `--strict`. Previously a
quiet repository could keep publishing a PDF that asserted a review which was really years
stale. The starter workflow also gained a weekly scheduled rebuild so the time-based "Review
overdue" badges stay current even on repositories that rarely push.

### A WCAG 2.1 AA-clean site

Every page type now passes automated axe-core WCAG 2.1/2.2 AA checks in both light and dark
mode, enforced by a new CI gate. That included real fixes: brand-colour contrast, underlined
in-prose links, keyboard-operable compliance-report sections that work without JavaScript,
ARIA-correct guide tabs and search, and a working "Skip to main content" link on every
template.

### Document classification, owners, and redaction banners

The PDF footer classification is now configurable (site-wide default or per policy), redacted
PDFs carry an in-document "REDACTED" title-page banner so a printed copy is self-identifying,
and policy pages finally render the document `owner` that was documented but shown nowhere.

See the "Building PDFs" guide to turn on tagged output for your own site.
